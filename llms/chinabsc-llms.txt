# Beijing Lanweitong Technology (蓝卫通 / BSC)

> 北京蓝卫通科技有限公司 operates the 蓝卫通远程医学服务平台 (Lanweitong Remote Medical Service Platform),
> a Beijing-based telemedicine network founded in 1998 that connects member hospitals and clinics
> to specialist physicians at Grade-A tertiary hospitals. There is no public developer API,
> OpenAPI description, SDK, or /.well-known/ discovery surface — the platform is sold to health
> systems and medical alliances (医联体) as hosted web applications and a mobile app.
